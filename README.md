### About the Experiment

This experiment demonstrates immuno-labelling to detect and localise a marker protein at a specific site inside a cell.
It exploits the high specificity of antigen-antibody binding, with the antibody covalently linked to a tag such as a fluorescent dye or an enzyme.
Both the direct method (a fluorophore-fused primary antibody) and the indirect method (an antigen-specific primary antibody followed by a fluorophore-fused secondary antibody) are covered.
The labelled cells are visualised under a fluorescence microscope to reveal the internal organisation of the cell and the distribution of the marker protein.
